BUY_LEG1_PRICE MUST BE LOWER THAN SELL_LEG2_PRICE

3. Mandatory Price Formulas
3.1 BUY_LEG1_PRICE
BUY_LEG1_PRICE =
    LEG1_AMOUNT_IN_A
    /
    LEG1_AMOUNT_OUT_B
Meaning:
How many units of starting asset A were paid for one unit of intermediate asset 
B.
This must be calculated from executable output, not spot price.

3.2 SELL_LEG2_PRICE
SELL_LEG2_PRICE =
    LEG2_AMOUNT_OUT_A
    /
    LEG2_AMOUNT_IN_B
Meaning:
How many units of starting asset A were received for one unit of intermediate 
asset B sold.
This must be calculated from executable output, not spot price.

BUY_LEG1_PRICE < SELL_LEG2_PRICE

LEG1_EXECUTABLE_PRICE < LEG2_EXECUTABLE_PRICE

18. Final Official Rule
MANDATORY LEG1 / LEG2 PRICE INVARIANT:
BUY_LEG1_PRICE < SELL_LEG2_PRICE
Expanded:
The system may only classify a route as executable arbitrage when LEG1 buys the 
intermediate asset at a lower executable price than LEG2 sells it for.
If the buy price is equal to or higher than the sell price, there is no 
executable price edge, no valid arbitrage opportunity, and no route submission.
Final compression:
BUY LOW ON LEG1.
SELL HIGHER ON LEG2.
REJECT EVERYTHING ELSE.
